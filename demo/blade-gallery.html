method, scope and provenance

b0 is the paired-identity gaussian baseline; b1 adds relighting; b2 adds full-gradient score consistency. l0 uses local/global synthesis; l1 adds relighting; l2 adds full-gradient normal consistency. r0 restricts that consistency gradient to the adapter. l3/r1/r2 include separate defect-lift variants; p0/p1 omit local/global synthesis. the complete ablation definitions remain in the experiment record.

global ascent and local focal supervision are adapted from glass (chen et al., eccv 2024). the whole gain over b0 cannot be attributed to routing alone. shared adapter updates can still change defect responses. this is not a full glass reproduction or a state-of-the-art claim.

this is one blade family, with 128 fitting images and 64 normal calibration images. the original source screen and full synthetic confirmation did not qualify. r0 was fixed before genuine-defect feedback and retained under an explicitly exploratory admission. this does not establish an improvement on the original eight-category ad2 study, which is evaluated separately. exact rgb identity cannot establish independent physical specimens.

paired confidence intervals resample exact-rgb image clusters while retaining all three seeds. they do not cover every source of training or specimen variation. images below are resized presentation copies; inference outputs were recorded separately and independently recounted.

no network connection or model computation occurs here. all 1,639 real images, 12 methods and three seeds remain in the audit; source/test overlap and conflicting labels are checked. dataset: aebad, zhang et al.; cc by 4.0. see the included dataset licence.
