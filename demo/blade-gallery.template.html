method and data

B0 uses Gaussian feature noise. B1 adds relighting; B2 adds coupled score consistency. L0 adds local/global synthesis; L1 adds relighting; L2 adds coupled normal consistency. R0 routes normal consistency through the adapter. L3/R1/R2 also use defect-lift variants; P0/P1 omit local/global synthesis.

Global ascent and local focal supervision are adapted from GLASS (Chen et al., ECCV 2024). Compare R0 with L1 to assess routing; the whole B0-to-R0 gain also includes the borrowed synthesis and supervision.

This exploratory study uses one blade family, 128 fit normals and 64 normal calibration images. The source screen and synthetic confirmation did not qualify. R0's pre-test selection is self-recorded. Background recall falls, and the stronger L0 control retains higher lighting recall.

Paired intervals resample exact-RGB image clusters across the three recorded seeds. They do not establish generalization to new seeds or physical specimens.
